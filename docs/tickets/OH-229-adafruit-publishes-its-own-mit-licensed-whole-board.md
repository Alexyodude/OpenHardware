    3405 ESP32 Feather HUZZAH/...                (not measured)

AP214, millimetres, exported by Autodesk Translation Framework (Fusion 360). No Feather ESP32-C6 model is there.

CLAUDE.md ranks geometry sources with "the vendor's own mechanical model" first, ahead of anything composed. The QT Py ESP32-C3 and the KB2040 are composed today from Adafruit's EAGLE board files, which draw the vendor's own copper, routing and silkscreen; a Fusion export of the assembly may carry none of those, or carry them as flat colour. So the ranking is not obviously the right answer for these two, and this ticket is the decision, not an assumption either way.

STEPS:
1. Fetch both STEPs (pinned by commit, size and sha256, gitignored, as tools/import_3d_model.py fetches others) and look at what each actually models: board outline and thickness, every part on it and which are named, whether copper, mask and silkscreen exist as bodies or colours, and how each placement compares with the composed model's (the same position, the same part?).
2. Look at both in the page side by side with the composed model (Playwright against webui/emulator_server.py), and say what each shows that the other does not.
3. Decide per board, with the evidence: draw the vendor's model, keep the composed one, or compose with the vendor's bodies substituted where the composer has none (OH-228 does that for the USB-C socket). Record the decision where the next reader will look -- the board's own manifest or model reason.

WHAT EACH FILE IS. AP214, one STEP Tools / Autodesk Translation Framework export, millimetres (SI_UNIT MILLI METRE), Z-up, origin at the board's lower-left corner, so the file's X,Y IS the EAGLE board frame (QT Py 17.780 x 20.701, KB2040 33.020 x 17.780); every instance sits at its element's own x, y and rotation from the .brd (X6 at 8.89,17.907 rot 180; KB2040 X6 at 2.6035,8.89 rot 270), lifted 1.57 mm for top parts and below z=0 for bottom parts. ONE solid is the PCB: z 0 to 1.570 mm (its geometry; the composer assumes 1.6), 102 faces (QT Py) / 149 (KB2040): two planes plus cylinders, i.e. the 14/26 castellation holes (r 0.5 on 2.54 pitch; KB2040 shallow r 0.699 scallops centred 0.25 outside the edge), the USB-C shell slots, ~40 vias cut as real r 0.197 holes, thru-hole pads r 0.3, and on the QT Py four true r 2.54 corners. It is UNSTYLED (no colour on any of its faces). There are NO copper, solder-mask, silkscreen or text bodies and no faces for them: 54% of the QT Py's 1,703 faces carry no colour at all (923; 1,369 on the KB2040), the other colours are about ten flat values on bodies (white/orange caps, black diodes, #656565 JST, #ffce7f USB contacts). The file holds 4 COLOUR_RGB entries. Products (one per package, named by package, id = the value of one placement): QT Py 17 (Board, 'PCB Component' = the assembly, 15 packages), 35 part instances; KB2040 18, 38 part instances. Named parts present: USB_C_CUSB31-CFM2AX-01-X, JST_SH4_SKINNY, QFN32_5MM, ANT_2450AT18B100, BTN_KMR2_*, SOT23-5, CRYSTAL_*, SOD-323_MINI / SOD-323F, SK6805_1515, 0402/0603/0805 families, QFN56_7MM_REDUCEDEPAD, USON8_4X4, CHIPLED_0603_NOOUTLINE. Bodies are crude: an 0402 is three boxes, the KB2040's 0603 caps are 0.10 mm slabs (composed 0.80), the 'SOD-323F' is 1.10 high where the composed is 0.73.

COMPARISON WITH THE COMPOSED MODEL (compose_board_3d.compose run with a spy on `place`, first call per element; the later calls are the half-turn checks and must not be read as the placement -- I got this wrong once and a screenshot caught it). By reference designator: QT Py 30 of 31 composed placements have a vendor body whose centre is within 0.2 mm in x,y (28 of them to 0.00 mm, both derive from the same .brd; the USB-C X6 is within 0.03 mm, which holds OH-228's placement and turn independently); KB2040 34 of 35 (X6 within 0.11 mm). The one miss on both boards is CONN1, the JST: 0.56 mm. It is the KiCad SM04B model centred on the pads against Adafruit's own skinny body; not settled which is right, Adafruit's own model against its own footprint is the stronger claim. Same package name for every placement. Heights differ by up to 0.4 mm on small parts (different models, not different positions). IN THE VENDOR FILE, NOT COMPOSED: QT Py LED1 (SK6805_1515, 'absent'), C7 and C9 (schematic values 'TBD'), L2 (refused: prefix says inductor, value says resistor); KB2040 LED1, U5 (USON8_4X4 QSPI flash, 'absent'), R8 (refused: 'FUSE'). The vendor draws C7, C9, L2, R8 as bodies, which settles nothing about whether they are fitted, so those four stay refused. VENDOR ONLY, NOT A PART: the true rounded QT Py outline (the .brd carries curve=-90 on four wires and the composer takes arcs as chords, eagle.py:346, so the composed QT Py has 45-degree chamfered corners where the board has r 2.54 -- a defect in the composed model, visible in shots/composed-qtpy-default.png and worth its own ticket), cut castellation half-holes and vias, and the stated 1.57 thickness. COMPOSED ONLY: every pad, 221+296 tracks and 36/47 vias as copper, the board's own silkscreen ink (text refused: EAGLE's vector font), the mask colour, the pad map the page lands pins on (the STEP has none).

DECISION, per board. QT Py ESP32-C3: COMPOSE WITH NAMED VENDOR BODIES SUBSTITUTED, not draw the vendor model. KB2040: the same. Evidence: the vendor model carries none of what CLAUDE.md says composed boards draw (copper, routing, silkscreen, pads) and leaves 54% of its faces unpainted, so ranking 'the vendor's own mechanical model' first would be a regression here; but it holds Adafruit's own body for the two packages the composer calls absent, which is what OH-228 did for the USB-C. WHAT TO SUBSTITUTE: SK6805_1515 (LED1, both boards; the two STEPs carry it identically, 1.5 x 1.5 x 0.48, 5 solids -- take it from the QT Py STEP already pinned for OH-228), USON8_4X4 (KB2040 U5; the KB2040 STEP is a second pinned file, 2,161,058 bytes / bc990ea6..., its own `pinned=` and its own row), and consider JST_SH4_SKINNY for CONN1 (0.56 mm). Do NOT substitute the four refused parts. Separately and cheaper: take the board thickness 1.57 from the STEP in place of the 1.6 assumption (a judgement on both rows, a statement here).
