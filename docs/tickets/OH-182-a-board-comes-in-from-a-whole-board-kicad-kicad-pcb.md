Part of OH-174, split out of OH-177. tools/import_board.py reads an EAGLE `.brd` and a KiCad `.kicad_mod` footprint (`import_board.READERS`), not a whole-board KiCad `.kicad_pcb`. Every XIAO came in as a footprint, and every manifest board so far is EAGLE, so nothing has needed it yet. The PCBCUPID Glyph Mini RP2040 does: its vendor publishes `glyph-mini-rp2040.kicad_pcb` (MIT) and no footprint of the board.

tools/kicad.py already parses `.kicad_pcb` for tools/compose_board_3d.py, which composes six XIAOs from Seeed's `.kicad_pcb` files. Reuse that parser. Selecting the header footprints by reference, as `--select` does for EAGLE elements, is the natural shape.

Pads must trace to the vendor's coordinates the way the EAGLE rows do, with the same position test. KiCad pads carry their net, so a pad's label is its net name as in the EAGLE rows. The Glyph Mini's nets are `/GPIO-n`: turning those into chip pins is OH-178's work, not this ticket's.

Done when a `.kicad_pcb` row imports with pad positions checked against the file, a `.kicad_pcb` naming a reference that is not there is refused naming it, and the Glyph Mini row exists once OH-181 lets its variant through (or this ticket says which of the two is still missing).

**Note 2026-09-29:** From OH-177's approving re-review, 2026-09-29, for whoever next edits tools/import_board.py: the merge in _from_eagle collapses the pads one schematic <connect> names into one pad, and nothing checks that those pads share a net. A connect like '1 1C 2 2C' would give 13 pads and silently drop one. Refuse a connect whose pads sit on more than one net, and make the KiCad path apply the same rule if a .kicad_pcb footprint maps one pin to several pads.

**Note 2026-09-29:** 2026-09-29: in review. `.kicad_pcb` is in `import_board.READERS`, read through `kicad.read_board`. `--select` names the header footprints by reference and is required: without it, the refusal lists every footprint that has through-hole pads. These are refused, naming the reference: a reference no footprint has or two footprints share ("0 footprints have the reference 'J9', and --select needs exactly one"), a reference named twice, a footprint with no through-hole pad, and `--symbols` or connects. A pad is labelled with its net; `kicad.Pad` gained `net`, read from `(net 29 "/GPIO-14")` or `(net "GND")` with the name last in both, and `""` in a `.kicad_mod`. A pad on no net keeps its number. Pads are placed by `kicad._on_board` (the footprint's position, rotation and side); KiCad's Y already runs down.
WHAT KICAD STATES ABOUT MULTI-PAD PINS: it does state it. KiCad joins a footprint's pads to its symbol's pins by pad number, so pads with the same number are one pin. In the Glyph file, every header pin is two thru_hole pads with one number and one `(pinfunction "Pin_n")`: a hole at local (0, y) and the castellated half-hole at (1.5, y). Both are on one net. The import merges them into one pad, kept at the first in the file, and the derivation gives the count. The EAGLE merge now follows the same rule: a schematic `<connect>` whose pads sit on two nets is refused, naming each pad and its net. Pads with one number on two nets are refused on the KiCad path the same way.
GLYPH MINI (`python tools/import_board.py third_party/pcbcupid-glyph-mini-2040/glyph-mini-rp2040.kicad_pcb --select J3 --select J4 --select J5`): the header references read from the file are J4 (PinHeader_1x09, at 0 degrees), J3 (PinHeader_1x05, at -90) and J5 (PinHeader_1x09, at 180). The fourth footprint with through-hole pads is J2, the USB-C socket (4 shield pads). The import gives 23 pads from 46, image 160 x 204 px, outline 18.00 x 23.50 mm. Labels: J4 /GPIO-0 to /GPIO-8; J3 /GPIO-9 to /GPIO-13; J5 /GPIO-14, /GPIO-15, /GPIO-26, /GPIO-27, /GPIO-28, /GPIO-29, +3V3, GND, /5V. The derivation reads "...; 23 pads were merged so." Every kept pad lies within 0.01 mm of a track end or via of its own net (the second pad of each pin is 1.5 mm off). With rotation dropped, 11 of 23 do. Groups: _grouped gives this U-shaped layout (two columns and a bottom row) header-column-1 to header-column-7, one per distinct x, so J3's five pads each get their own column. That comes from the existing grouping and was not changed here. It matters when the row is written. Without `--select`, the run exits 2 listing J5, J2, J3 and J4; `--select J9` exits 2 naming J9. The manifest row is NOT added: tools/board_manifest.py belongs to OH-178, and the Glyph's variant waits on OH-181. Both are still missing, so this ticket's third done-criterion is open.
