Follow-ups from OH-216 and its two reviews (2026-10-09). None changes what the page draws for any board today.

1. The 25 XIAO board files carry OH-216's `underside` groups, written in once and held by a test, but their `derivation` still says "Generated by tools/import_kicad_board.py" and nothing about `underside`, and their `header-top`/`header-bottom` groups come from that retired importer; today's `tools/import_board.py` writes `header-left`/`header-right` or `header-row-N`. Regenerate the XIAO files with `tools/import_board.py --module` so the importer's own sentence reaches them, held by a test that the committed files reproduce exactly. Check what reads those group names before changing them (OH-216's review found only board3d.js and tests).
2. Fetched SMD meshes are still centred on every pad, underside ones included, so their castellation metal sits about 0.3 mm off its pads (ESP32-S3 +0.315 mm, nRF52840 -0.306 mm). Centring on topside pads alone would fix the SAMD21, RP2040 and S3 SMDs but push the nRF52840 further off, because its NFC1/NFC2 pads' side is not stated (Seeed publishes no board file for that chip). State `header_pads` for fetched meshes, or the nRF52840's NFC pads' side, and centre on pads both sides name.
3. `tools/import_board.py` takes `pads_of("thru_hole") or pads_of("smd")`, which drops Seeed's SMD-numbered castellations 1-14 on the XIAO-nRF54LM20A-DIP footprint; that board file has no header pins at all, only underside test points, and the page centres its fetched mesh on them.
4. A refusal in `placeModel` (no turn lands the pads; names disagree; no PCB thickness) is thrown inside `loadModel`, which is not awaited, so it reaches only the browser console: the slab stays and nothing on the page says why. Show it.
5. `stated_header_pads` in `tools/compose_board_3d.py` approximates which pads the importer keeps (the importer keeps through-hole pads, one per number and net, and every pad with no net; the composer keeps SMD pads of header elements and drops a castellated half-hole with no net beside a whole hole). No current board differs; a future one would be refused, not misplaced. Derive both from one rule.
6. From the confirm review, LOW: a module with fewer than NAME_MARGIN named pins under it can never be read with `--module`, and the refusal reads as if the turns were ambiguous (say so in its own message and in `--module`'s help); the margin's value, case-folding and the `/` split of pin names are held by no test; `>=` for `>` in the names-disagree check would refuse a model whose names share nothing with its board, which the docstring says the outline places; `board3d.js`'s "One such pad is not that" reads as if a single crossed pad were always tolerated; `_under_the_module`'s docstring leaves out "nearest". The C5-SMD clears the margin by one pin, partly on power and enable names.

Done when the XIAO files regenerate exactly from today's importer, every fetched mesh is centred on pads both sides name, the nRF54LM20A-DIP has its header pins, a placement refusal is shown on the page, and the lows are fixed or each says why not.
